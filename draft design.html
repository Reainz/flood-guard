Active
River level at Tân Châu is 3.8m and rising at 8cm/hr. Forecast models indicate flood arrival within 68–74 hours affecting low-lying agricultural land in Tân Châu and Châu Phú districts.
Crop-specific recommendations
🌾
Rice (grain-filling): Assess harvest readiness immediately. If panicles are 80%+ filled, emergency harvest may be preferable to flood damage.
🥬
Vegetables: Harvest all mature produce immediately. Move seedlings to elevated ground or raised beds.
🏠
Safety: Prepare emergency kit. Know evacuation route to nearest shelter (Tân Châu Community Center, 2.3 km NE).
